# The Q2D name

"Q2D" and "Query-to-Data" identify this protocol project. The specification and
reference code are openly licensed and free to implement; this policy is only
about the *name*, so that a reader can tell where a claim about Q2D came from.

Nothing here restricts your right to implement the protocol, fork the code, or
adapt the documents under their respective licenses.

## You may, without asking

- State that your software **implements Q2D**, is **compatible with Q2D**, or
  **supports the Q2D protocol**.
- Name the version or profile you implement — "implements Q2D 0.1, authenticated-answer profile".
- Use the name in prose, articles, talks, and academic work.
- Link to `q2d.dev` and the specification.

## Please do not

- Use "Q2D" as, or as part of, your product or company name, or in a logo or
  domain that suggests the project produced or endorsed it.
- State or imply that an implementation is **certified**, **verified**,
  **compliant**, or **conformant** in an official sense. **There is no
  conformance program and no certification mark.** A conformance suite is
  planned; until it exists and has been independently run, no such claim is
  meaningful.
- Describe a modified protocol as "Q2D" without saying plainly what you changed.
  The specification's claim and non-claim language is load-bearing — see
  `spec/terminology.md` §9. Statements Q2D deliberately does not make should not
  reappear under its name.

## Status

The name is used but not registered. This policy documents intended use; it is
not a legal instrument, and the project asserts no rights beyond those arising
from use. A certification mark is deferred until independent implementations and
neutral governance exist — a self-created "Q2D Verified" mark before then would
read as commercial control rather than governance.

Questions about a use not covered here: open an issue.
